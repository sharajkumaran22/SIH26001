⚠️ CURRENT PROTOTYPE BOUNDARIES

Transparent technical disclosures for SIH evaluation

DECISION SUPPORT ONLY
  • Decision-Support Prototype: This application does not claim to predict the exact minute, hour, or pinpoint boundary of a slope failure. It provides risk probabilities and situational intelligence to assist field officers.
  • Demonstration & Simulated Datasets: Because live IMD radar and state-wide in-situ geotechnical telemetry are currently restricted or unavailable without formal MoES/NDMA API credentials, environmental readings are seeded as demonstration values.
  • Weighted Prototype Model: The primary risk scoring uses a normalized weighted model (Rainfall 35%, Soil Moisture 25%, Slope 20%, Ground Movement 20%) designed for immediate explainability and deterministic behavior during testing.
  • Simulated IoT Ground Movement: Displacement values represent simulated borehole inclinometer readings calibrated to standard slope failure mechanics.
  • Field Photo Computer Vision: The field photo module provides operational proof-of-concept for inspector uploads, with automated crack detection and satellite change detection staged for future phases.

🚀 FUTURE PRODUCTION SYSTEM

Scalable milestones for post-hackathon pilot deployment

ROADMAP
  • Regional Geotechnical ML Calibration: Ingest historical landslide inventories from Geological Survey of India (GSI) Bhukosh to train localized XGBoost and Deep Learning models for each Himalayan geological belt.
  • Live IMD AWS & Radar Integration: Connect directly to India Meteorological Department API feeds for 3-hour nowcasting and precipitation accumulation.
  • Low-Cost Solar IoT Sensor Nodes: Deploy ESP32-based telemetry nodes equipped with MEMS accelerometers, capacitive soil moisture probes, and tipping-bucket rain gauges transmitting via LoRaWAN / SATCOM.
  • Copernicus Sentinel-1 InSAR: Integrate Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar (InSAR) surface displacement mapping to detect millimeter-level ground deformation over inaccessible mountain slopes.
  • Automated Multi-Channel Broadcast: Integrate CAP (Common Alerting Protocol) for automated SMS, WhatsApp, and siren broadcasts to local village disaster management committees (VDMC).
